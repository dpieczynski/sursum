# Texts considered and left out

Texts that were looked at for this library and deliberately not included, with the reason. Being
wrong about copyright is far worse than having fewer songs, so anything uncertain stays out.
"Not done" entries are public domain but were left out for another reason (unverified
transcription, liturgical form rather than a hymn, out of scope); they can be added later by
checking the scan.

## Copyright or source reasons

| Text | Source looked at | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Any hymn taken from a modern songbook or website (e.g. current editions of Siedlecki's *Śpiewnik kościelny*, *Śpiewnik liturgiczny*, spiewnik websites) | – | Modern editions may carry protected editorial changes; texts were taken only from editions of 1838–1849. Modern sites were used only to find titles. |
| Ks. J. Siedlecki, *Śpiewnik kościelny* (1878 and later) | web search for digitized early editions | No early edition with a usable text layer was found in the time available; post-war editions are not acceptable. Not used. |
